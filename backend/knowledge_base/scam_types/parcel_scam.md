# Scam Type: Parcel Scam

## Category

Delivery Scam


## Description

Parcel scam is a type of fraud where scammers pretend to be delivery companies or courier services.

They usually send fake SMS messages claiming that a parcel cannot be delivered due to unpaid fees, incomplete information, or delivery issues.


## Common Attack Methods

- Fake courier SMS messages
- Fake delivery websites
- Requests for small payment fees
- Fake tracking links
- Collection of personal information


## Common Keywords

- Parcel
- Delivery
- Tracking
- Failed delivery
- Delivery fee
- Pay now
- Update address
- Confirm details
- Courier


## Common Indicators

The message may contain:

- Unexpected parcel notifications
- Requests for small payments
- Suspicious tracking URLs
- Urgent deadlines
- Unknown sender


## Example Scam Message

Example:

"Your parcel delivery failed. Please pay RM2 delivery fee and update your address through the following link."


## Why This Is Dangerous

Attackers create fake delivery websites to collect:

- Credit card information
- Personal details
- Banking information

Victims may also install malicious applications from fake links.


## Prevention

- Do not click unknown delivery links.
- Check delivery information through official courier applications.
- Do not provide payment information to unknown websites.
- Verify the sender before taking action.


## Related Scam Types

- Phishing Scam
- Fake Website Scam
- Payment Scam
