# Scam Type: Bank Scam

## Category

Financial Scam


## Description

Bank scam is a type of fraud where attackers pretend to be bank representatives or financial institutions to steal users' personal information, banking credentials, or money.

Scammers commonly use SMS messages, phone calls, fake websites, or phishing links to deceive victims.


## Common Attack Methods

- Fake bank SMS messages
- Fake customer service calls
- Phishing websites
- Fake mobile banking applications
- Requests for OTP or account verification


## Common Keywords

- OTP
- Verify account
- Account suspended
- Security update
- Login
- Transaction
- Suspicious activity
- Click here
- Urgent action required


## Common Indicators

The message may contain:

- Urgent requests requiring immediate action
- Threats of account suspension or blocking
- Suspicious URLs
- Requests for confidential information
- Requests for OTP, password, or banking details


## Example Scam Message

Example:

"Your bank account has been suspended due to suspicious activity. Please verify your account immediately by clicking the link below."


## Why This Is Dangerous

Attackers use fake messages and websites to collect users' login credentials, personal information, or financial details.

Victims may lose money or have their bank accounts compromised.


## Prevention

- Never share OTP or password with anyone.
- Do not click unknown links received through SMS.
- Verify information through official banking applications.
- Contact the bank using official channels.


## Related Scam Types

- Phishing Scam
- Identity Theft
- Fake Website Scam
