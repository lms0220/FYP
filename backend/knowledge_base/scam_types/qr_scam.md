# Scam Type: QR Scam

## Category
Payment Scam

## Description
QR scams use fake QR codes to redirect victims to malicious websites or payment requests.

## Common Keywords
- Scan QR
- Payment
- Discount
- Reward

## Common Indicators
- Unknown QR codes
- Suspicious payment requests

## Prevention
- Verify QR source
- Do not scan unknown QR codes
